# Shop Owner Phase 5 Capability Retirement Matrix

## Scope and decision rules

This is a freeze of the legacy owner ERP callers and data surfaces, not a new
authorization registry. It was characterized from the current route table,
`routes/shop-owner-erp.php`, `routes/shop-owner-erp-api.php`, and the caller
inventory. The authority for the seven approval rows is the Phase 4
[approval matrix](shop-owner-phase-4-approval-matrix.md). The generated
[ERP route matrix](shop-owner-erp-route-matrix.md) is a cross-check only;
where it differs from an executable owner route, this document records the
route and requires the capability test named below before retirement.

`OWNER_READABLE` means the owner may receive the listed shop-scoped record
set. `CONDITIONAL` means the route is only a page launcher or needs the
listed module/record capability; it is not a permission grant. `OWNER_DENIED`
means the owner must not receive the underlying staff/audit/sensitive data.
Module visibility never changes those classifications.

## Individual owner operation boundary

Individual owners retain the owner-operated retail and repair workflow,
including the canonical Payments landing and its Retail/Repair POS pages.
Company owners use the staff/cashier POS surface instead, so the owner POS
routes are individual-only. Logistics is intentionally company-only because
individual shops rely on third-party delivery and do not operate the
dispatcher/rider workflow.

## Revision notice (2026-08-25)

The revised Phase 5 requirement keeps owner-safe employee/workforce pages in
scope and includes a read-only Attendance view. The `hr.attendance` row below
is `OWNER_READABLE` for the tenant-scoped read projection. Attendance
mutation/API rows remain `OWNER_DENIED` and must not appear in owner
navigation. This note does not grant the owner employee self-service,
time-in/time-out, edit, delete, or other attendance operations.

## Canonical Dashboard and local-page decisions

`Dashboard` is the owner-facing landing for every enabled module. Existing
owner-safe metrics are reused as a small server-derived summary inside the
canonical Dashboard; they are not exposed as a second owner tab. Staff and
product-handler actors keep their existing employee dashboards and navigation.

| Module | Owner Dashboard content | Owner local pages | Owner-specific actions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Retail | products and order counts | Products, Orders | existing owner-authorized retail actions only |
| Repair | open repair jobs and service count from the existing repair read models | Repair Services | Create Repair Service; service API is tenant-scoped |
| Workforce | employee, active employee, and pending leave counts | Employees, read-only Attendance | Create Employee; no attendance mutation or employee self-service |
| Finance | invoice and expense review counts | Invoices, Expenses | Read-only; no Create Invoice or Create Expense |
| Customers / CRM | customer orders and review counts | Customers, Customer Reviews | existing owner-safe reads |
| Inventory | product, low-stock, and supplier-order counts | Product Inventory, Stock Movement, Supplier Order Monitoring | Supplier Order Monitoring is read-only to the owner |
| Procurement | purchase request and purchase order counts | Purchase Requests, Purchase Orders, Suppliers | existing characterized procurement permissions |
| Logistics | shipment and batch counts | Shipments, Batches, Riders | Batches remain governed by the existing batch-management policy |
