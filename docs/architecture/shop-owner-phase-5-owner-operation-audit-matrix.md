operation. `Denied-attempt behavior` is part of the contract: a hidden link

## Resolution checkpoint

The formerly unresolved material-operation rows are explicitly excluded from
Phase 5 owner operations. Route/module visibility is not evidence for POS,
uploads, staff/HR actions, audit exports, correction flows, or protected record
access. Their direct requests fail closed, no owner success audit is emitted,
and their legacy callers remain documented as retirement/compatibility evidence.
